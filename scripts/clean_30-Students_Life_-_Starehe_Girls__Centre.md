## Students Life


Student life at Starehe Girls Centre is vibrant and enriching. The school provides a nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence, personal growth, and leadership development. Students are actively engaged in a wide range of extracurricular activities, including sports, music, drama, and community service. The school also places a strong emphasis on discipline and character building, instilling values of integrity, responsibility, and respect in its students. The sense of sisterhood and camaraderie among the students creates a supportive and inclusive community where everyone is encouraged to reach their full potential. Overall, Starehe Girls Centre offers a holistic education that prepares young women to become confident, compassionate, and empowered individuals ready to make a positive impact in the world.
