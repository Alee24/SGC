# Principal


### Welcome Message from the Principal


Dear Future Students, Parents, and Valued Educational Partners,


I extend a warm welcome to Starehe Girls Centre, a beacon of academic excellence, leadership, and holistic development, with a sense of immense pride and pleasure. I am pleased to provide updates that demonstrate our unwavering dedication to the development of resilient, innovative, and compassionate young women as we celebrate our accomplishments and seize new opportunities.


KCSE Performance


For the last three years Starehe Girls Centre has been registering positive deviation in KCSE in the year 2025 it registered a mean score 8.90 (B) with a positive deviation of 0.07. In the year 2024 it registered a mean score of 8.83 (B) with a positive deviation of 0.34.


This positive deviation is attributed to different strategies that motivates both the teachers and the students to work hard. One of the strategies is “Operation Plus 5” This is a self-evaluation Slogan in which every student targets to add at least 5 marks for every subject from last exam done. This targets that the student move at least to the next grade. For every exam done, the Schools majors on awarding most improved so that every student has a chance of being awarded.


2026 Candidates have mean score target of 9.50 and everybody is working hard towards that.


University Transition


In the year 2025 out 187 candidates, 93% scored a C+ (plus) and above, only 7% students got C plain and below. Our target for 2026 candidates is 100% transition to the University.


Strategies


Our students receives individualized rigorous academic performance and mentorship facilitated by a renowned academic mentors. Our students are also offered psychosocial support to ensure success not only in examination but also future change makers.


Seamless CBC Transition Preparations


As Kenya advances with the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), Starehe Girls Centre leads the way in this transformative journey. Currently the School is making significant investments in teacher training, upgrade our infrastructure, and introduced innovative learning tools to align with CBC’s focus on creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving. We aim to provide students with the necessary skills to thrive in a changing world, all while upholding our fundamental principles of discipline and integrity towards all people, through dedicated service to our nation, with head, heart, and hand committed to justice, zeal, and care.


Join Our Family in 2027: A Sanctuary for Learning


To potential parents looking for a quality education for their girls, we encourage you to enroll at Starehe Girls Centre beginning in 2027. Our campus, nestled in a tranquil, secure location, offers the ideal setting for intellectual advancement, artistic expression, and spiritual development. Students thrive here, surrounded by abundant nature, cutting-edge facilities, and a loving community that believes in each girl’s potential. The School also admits 30% of able students who are self-sponsored.


Our Promise: Holistic Excellence


In addition to academics, we emphasize leadership, character development, and community engagement. Through clubs, sports, and partnerships, we shape individuals who are prepared to lead with compassion and bravery.


As Principal, I find myself continually motivated by the determination and intelligence of our students. Alongside our committed team and stakeholders, we continue to uphold our mission to provide care and education for girls in need and inspire them to transform into productive and exemplary members of society.


Explore Starehe Girls Centre


We invite you to explore our campus, connect with our lively community, and experience the essence of the Starehe spirit firsthand. For inquiries or enrollment details regarding the 2027 intake, please reach out to us at 0707900852 or Info@starehegirlscentre.co.ke.


Thank you for trusting us with your daughters’ futures. At Starehe, we don’t just educate girls—we ignite legacies.


“Elimu Yetu, Nguvu Yetu” | Our Education, Our Strength


Yours in service,


Beatrice Shirao


Principal
