# Placement KUCCPS


## INFORMATION TO KCSE CLASS OF 2024


Tuesday, January 28, 2025


To the KCSE Class of 2024Starehe Girls’ Centre


Dear Esteemed Class of 2024,


### RE: CONGRATULATIONS ON YOUR KCSE SUCCESS AND UNIVERSITY PLACEMENT INFORMATION


We are delighted to take this opportunity to congratulate you on your improved excellent performance in the KCSE 2024 examinations. Your hard work, commitment, and resilience have yielded remarkable results, and the entire Starehe Girls’ Centre community is immensely proud of you.


Based on KCSE 2024 performance, the school is projecting an impressive 95% university entry rate. This is a testament to your dedication and the collective support you have received from your teachers, parents, and peers. We celebrate this significant milestone and are confident that you will continue to excel in your future academic and professional endeavors.


As you prepare for the next phase of your academic journey, we wish to remind you that the Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) portal is currently open and if you are interested in applying to KMTC/TVET, the portal is currently accepting applications. Kindly visit https://students.kuccps.net/ to explore available courses and make your applications.


We strongly advise you to keep checking the KUCCPS website regularly for updates on placement opportunities, changes, and deadlines to ensure that you maximize your chances of securing a spot in a course and institution of your choice.


Should you experience any challenges during the placement process, please do not hesitate to reach out to Starehe Girls Centre for assistance. Our IT Office staff will be readily available to guide and support you in navigating the KUCCPS platform or addressing any technical issues.


Once again, congratulations on this remarkable achievement. As you step into the next chapter of your life, we encourage you to remain focused, purposeful, confident in your abilities and remember, wherever you go, carry with you the good name of Starehe Girls Centre. The future is bright, and we believe you will continue to make us proud.


With warm regards and best wishes,


Yours sincerely,


Sr. Jane SoitaDIRECTOR
